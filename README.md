# Premium Cosmetic Laser Center – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

Premium Cosmetic Laser Center is a healthcare and aesthetic clinic established in 2015, located in Jumeirah, Dubai. The clinic provides dermatology, cosmetic laser treatments, aesthetic medicine and general and specialist dental services.

The redesigned homepage focuses on presenting the clinic as a specialist-led destination for skin, laser and dental treatments. The direction prioritises clearer service communication, easier treatment discovery and stronger visibility of the medical team.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Stronger Homepage Messaging
- Replaced the generic welcome message with a more descriptive headline communicating the clinic's skin, laser and dental services.
- Improved the introduction to highlight its combined dermatology, aesthetic and dental expertise.
- Made the clinic's core service offering clearer from the first section.

**Problem addressed:** The previous headline, "Welcome to Premium Skin Laser Center," did not adequately communicate the clinic's full range of services or establish a distinctive value proposition.

### 2. Improved Service Discovery
- Organised treatments into clearer categories covering dermatology, aesthetics, laser treatments and dentistry.
- Simplified the presentation of individual treatment options.
- Improved navigation pathways to help visitors identify relevant services more easily.

**Problem addressed:** Extensive nested navigation and a broad treatment list made it harder for visitors to find the services relevant to their needs.

### 3. Greater Visibility for Specialist Credentials
- Improved the presentation of the clinic's identified specialists.
- Gave greater prominence to professional roles and medical specialisms.
- Connected specialist information more effectively with the clinic's treatment offerings.

**Problem addressed:** The previous homepage did not prominently introduce individual specialists and their qualifications within the main visitor journey, limiting opportunities to establish clinical trust.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual Presentation:** Created a more polished and professional aesthetic healthcare appearance.
- **Layout:** Improved the homepage structure and separation of treatment categories.
- **Typography:** Strengthened content hierarchy to distinguish primary messaging, section headings and supporting information.
- **User Experience:** Simplified treatment discovery and improved access to relevant service information.
- **Consistency:** Established a more cohesive presentation across dermatology, aesthetics, dentistry and specialist sections.
- **Overall Appearance:** Positioned the clinic's combined skin, laser and dental expertise more clearly throughout the homepage.

## Project Objective

The redesigned homepage aims to communicate Premium Cosmetic Laser Center's full range of specialist services, improve treatment discovery and strengthen patient confidence through clearer presentation of its medical team and expertise.
